BookBridge 7.10.0 (digest `sha256:4ae92f09…18e2d6`), throwaway container on thebigbox (`--network none`, scratch SQLite, nothing live touched). Script `spikes/s9_mass_delete.py`, result `S9.json`.

## What happened
1. Device A uploads 10 highlights with a complete key list, acks. Device B pulls (10 adds) and acks.
2. Device A then sends `keysComplete: true` with **zero keys** (a wiped or reset sidecar, E8).
3. Hub result: **10 tombstones, 0 live annotations.** Device B's next exchange is told to **delete all 10**. No confirmation, no threshold, no undo besides re-upload.

So one device with an empty sidecar and a "complete" flag erases a book's annotations everywhere. Same risk class as the mass-delete issue the owner already knows from other sync tools.

## Guard (R10) to build in Phase 3
- Hub refuses to tombstone more than max(3, 25 %) of a book's live annotations from one exchange; it parks the request in the review queue (Task 23) and alerts on Telegram instead of applying.
- A key list of zero keys with `keysComplete` on a book that had annotations is always parked.
- Tombstones stay recoverable (soft delete, `deleted_at`) for 30 days; a "restore" action in the review queue.
