# S3 — BookBridge alignment on one paired title (2026-10-21)

**Result: PASS.** 10/10 random timestamps matched (gate: 9/10). Book: *This Is How You Lose the Time War*
(209,197 chars, 28 mp3 = 15,359 s = 4.27 h).

## Setup (throwaway, nothing live touched)
- Image `ghcr.io/cporcellijr/bookbridge@sha256:4ae92f09dc98526ffa0a63f1ea1e31bd36876da64deb8d8948e70acd0e18e2d6` (v7.10.0, index digest), pulled on thebigbox.
- Container ran with `--network none --cpus 4 --memory 6g`, no ports, no ABS/Kavita/KOSync contact; only copies of the EPUB and the 28 mp3s in
  `/media/containers/readsync-spikes/s3/` (kept for S9).
- Driver `spikes/s3_align_run.py` calls BookBridge's own `AlignmentService.align_and_store` on a scratch SQLite DB.

## Numbers
| Step | Where | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Transcribe 4.27 h audio, whisper.cpp large-v3-turbo, word timestamps | Jadu GPU1 | **290 s** (~53x realtime) |
| Alignment (lexical n-gram map from transcript) | thebigbox CPU, 4 cores | **14.3 s**, peak RSS 154 MB |
| Map | | 26,271 anchors, **coverage 1.0** (last char = text end), last ts 15,354.6 s of 15,359 s |

Largest gaps between anchors: 52.8 s at the end (outro), 43.9 s at the start (intro/credits), 24 s mid-book (~5,731 s). B12 is fine for this book.
So the "alignment takes hours on CPU" worry (S3 gate in the plan, >3 h) does not apply: the slow step is transcription, and that is minutes on the GPU.

## Accuracy (spikes/s3_alignment_eval.py, seed 7)
10 random 20 s clips -> Whisper -> `rapidfuzz.partial_ratio` against the text the map places at that time (±3 s slack): scores 88.4–98.2, mean 94.9, all ≥ 80.
Negative control (same clips against text 10 min away): 41.7–47.5. The metric discriminates. Per-sample rows: `S3/eval.json`.
Caveat: `partial_ratio` against a ~26 s window is lenient about exact word position; it proves the map is right to within roughly a few sentences, not to the word. Fine for bookmark placement (sentence level), not proof of word-level follow-along.

## Findings that change the plan
1. **The Whisper shim (`:9092`) drops timestamps.** It returns `{"text": ...}` only, even with `response_format=verbose_json`. BookBridge's `WHISPER_CPP_URL` provider
   therefore degrades to one segment with no timing ("alignment will be degraded"). Direct whisper.cpp `:9093/inference` returns segments with word times, but is loopback-only on Jadu.
   Task 17 must either: extend the shim to pass through `verbose_json` (preferred; small change on Jadu, update the Whisper Shim vault note), or run the transcribe step on Jadu as in `s3_transcribe_jadu.py`.
   The shim is fine for S4 (clip -> text only).
2. The standard image has no torch, so the CTC forced aligner is not available; the default transcript-based path was enough here. CTC (`-ctc` image) stays optional.
3. Wall time per book is dominated by transcription; ~5 min per 4 h of audio on GPU1. GPU1 is shared with other jobs (Whisper reserve, llm-gate): schedule alignments as lane-deferred work.
